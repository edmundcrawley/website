How to use this page

  • Find your cello number, then open your part (PDF) and print it. Parts marked Beginner have note names and finger numbers on every note. Parts marked Expert include thumb position.
  • Practice tracks play your part loud with everyone else quiet, after one bar of clicks to count you in. Start with the slow version.
  • The audio is computer-generated, so use it for notes and timing, not for tone.
  • If you'd rather practise offline, tap a track and use the player's ⋮ menu (or right-click the button) to download it.

Suggested running order

Carol of the Bells → Deck the Halls → Russian Dance. Open with the piece everyone knows and that holds together most easily. Put the singalong in the middle. Finish on the fast, loud ending.

All three pieces are traditional or by long-dead composers (Leontovych, Tchaikovsky). The arrangements were made for this concert. The full scores are in the conductor's PDFs.
