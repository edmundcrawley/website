Photographs to check

The Imperial War Museum holds a series of wartime photographs of SOE officers and Cretan partisans taken in 1944–45, from the collection of Lieutenant Hugh Fraser (“Levtheri”), an SOE officer who went about Crete disguised as a shepherd. The captions that could be recovered name Leigh Fermor, Moss, Fielding, Barnes, Psychoundakis and the Bandouvas band, and the places they give are in western and central Crete, Fraser’s own ground, not Sandy’s Lasithi. Nothing in them names Sandy. But about half the series has no caption online, and these men met and photographed one another, so a family member who knows his face is the only one who can rule him out. This page gathers what is known so the looking is easy.

How to look. Each entry links to the photograph’s own page at the Imperial War Museum, where it can be seen full size. The museum’s site also lists the whole Fraser collection (89 negatives, Crete and Malaya): collection record, and a gallery of every image. The catalogue numbers run from about HU 66036 to HU 66097; the ones below are those whose captions could be read. If you think you see Sandy, note the HU number and tell Edmund.

Notes

  • Why Sandy is unlikely to be here, and why it is still worth looking. Hugh Fraser worked in western and central Crete; every place his captions name (the White Mountains, Selino, Mount Kedros, the Bandouvas band’s country round Viannos) is well west of Lasithi. Sandy’s own photographs, if he took any, would have gone home with him. But the British officers gathered at Dunbabin’s headquarters and at the liberation in 1945, and the series runs to February 1945, so an uncaptioned frame could show him.
  • People who appear more than once: Steve Gillespie (wireless operator), Lt Cavil Peacock, Kapetan Manoli Bandouvas (“Bo Peep”) and his brothers, Patrick Leigh Fermor (“Michalaki”), Xan Fielding (“Aleko”), Bill Moss (“Demetrios”), Dick Barnes (“Pavlo”), George Psychoundakis (author of The Cretan Runner), Manolis Paterakis.
  • The museum’s dating. Only HU 66045 carries a date (2 June 1944); the rest are catalogued within the series’ span of June 1944 to February 1945.
  • Catalogue numbers without a readable caption (in the series but not described online): HU 66036, 66053, 66057, 66083, and probably most numbers between 66036 and 66097 not listed above. These are the ones to look at.
  • Other photograph sources checked and empty: Wikimedia Commons, the Getty, Alamy and TopFoto agencies, the National Portrait Gallery, the Leigh Fermor blog’s “SOE colleagues” gallery, and the Greek sites about the cave.
